# financial_conditions

**Question:** Do financial market and credit conditions support tighter or easier policy?

Loose financial conditions that offset tightening are hawkish (+): the Committee needs more restraint. Tight or stressed conditions, or financial stability concerns, are dovish (-).

## Where to look

Keywords: "financial conditions," "equity prices," "credit spreads," "Treasury yields," "term premium," "bank lending," "credit availability," "liquidity," "market functioning," "financial stability," "volatility," "stress," "dollar."
Sections: Developments in Financial Markets; Staff Review of the Financial Situation; Participants' Views.

## Anchors

| Score | What the minutes look like |
|---|---|
| +1.00 | Conditions very accommodative despite tightening; participants say they are undermining policy and must be offset |
| +0.75 | Loose conditions that "most" see as working against the stance |
| +0.50 | Easing conditions; many note they reduce restraint |
| +0.25 | Slightly easier conditions; a few mention it |
| 0.00 | Conditions stable and roughly consistent with the stance |
| -0.25 | Modest tightening; some see it as doing some of the policy work |
| -0.50 | Clear tightening in credit or markets; many see it as slowing activity |
| -0.75 | Significant stress; most express concern about financial stability or credit availability |
| -1.00 | Market dysfunction or severe stress; participants focus on liquidity support or stability |

## Sub-signals

1. Equity and bond market moves and the level of yields.
2. Credit spreads and bank lending standards.
3. Market functioning, liquidity, and any strain episodes.
4. Whether participants describe conditions as reinforcing or offsetting the policy stance.

## Pitfalls

- The sign depends on policy stance, not on whether markets did well. A rally during tightening is hawkish; a rally during easing is neutral to slightly hawkish.
- Financial stability concerns can pull the score negative even while inflation is high. Weigh participants' emphasis.
- Do not use external market data. Use what the minutes describe.
