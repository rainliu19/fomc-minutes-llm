# inflation

**Question:** Do participants' views on inflation and inflation expectations support tighter or easier policy?

Score the policy implication. High, persistent, or broadening inflation is hawkish (+). Falling, well-contained inflation is dovish (-).

## Where to look

Keywords: "inflation," "PCE," "core," "price pressures," "disinflation," "persistent," "elevated," "expectations," "anchored," "services inflation," "tariffs," "pass-through," "price stability mandate."
Sections: Staff Review of the Economic Situation; Participants' Views.

## Anchors

| Score | What the minutes look like |
|---|---|
| +1.00 | Inflation far above target and broadening; expectations at risk of de-anchoring; participants express alarm |
| +0.75 | Inflation well above target and persistent; most see upside risk and a need for restrictive policy |
| +0.50 | Inflation above target with slow progress; many worry it is not coming down fast enough |
| +0.25 | Inflation somewhat above target or progress stalled; a few concerns |
| 0.00 | Inflation near target or movement mixed; participants split on persistence |
| -0.25 | Inflation near target with modest downside concern; some note easing pressures |
| -0.50 | Clear progress; most see inflation moving toward target; little concern about re-acceleration |
| -0.75 | Inflation below target or falling fast; many note risk of persistent undershoot |
| -1.00 | Deflation risk or a sharp inflation collapse; participants stress that inflation is too low |

## Sub-signals

Combine these, weighing the one participants emphasize most.

1. Level versus the 2 percent goal.
2. Direction and momentum (recent monthly readings).
3. Breadth (goods, services, housing).
4. Expectations (survey and market-based measures, and whether they are "anchored").

## Pitfalls

- Temporary factors (one-time price level shifts) described as "transitory" by "many" pull toward 0.
- Tariff or supply shocks that participants describe as raising prices once versus persistently matter. Score the persistence claim.
- Staff descriptions of data and participants' interpretations can differ. Weigh participants' views over the staff review when they conflict, and say so.
